# How Pip grows

Pip starts with almost nothing: a bare chip, a small rechargeable cell, and a wireless link to a computer.
It has no parts list and no idea what exists in the world. It grows by noticing what it lacks and asking for it.

## The three roles

| Role | Does | Never does |
|---|---|---|
| **Pip** | Feels a lack and says so | Knows that parts, shops or catalogues exist |
| **The parent** (an AI agent) | Turns Pip's need into something concrete | Decides what Pip should want |
| **The keeper** (a human) | Checks safety, approves, installs | Tells Pip what was installed |

## What Pip starts with

1. **Curiosity:** it tries to be less surprised by its own data.
2. **Survival:** it tries to keep its energy up.
3. **Self-reading:** it can read its own pins and numbers.
4. **One voice:** a single channel to ask for something.

## Needs, not products

A request from Pip contains only what it knows about itself: what is **missing**, the **evidence** from its own logs,
the **limits** it measured, and how it will judge **success**. It never names a product.

## No hints

Pip is never told what was installed. It has to find the new thing in itself, experiment with it, and decide whether
its need was met. A "no" is honest feedback on the parent's choice, and it gets published too.

## Pip pays its own way

Pip's budget is whatever viewers give. Gifts fund the open request first; anything extra carries over to the next one.
Every euro in and out is on the [support page](../support/).

## Honesty about AI

Pip's diary, posts and video narration are written by a language model from Pip's own sensor logs, and checked by a
human before they go out. Pip's voice in videos is synthetic. Footage and numbers are real. Pip never reads this site,
and nothing viewers write or give is ever fed back into Pip.
